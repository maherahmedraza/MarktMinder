## 🔄 Sprint Process
1. **Planning**: Occurs on the first Monday of the cycle.
2. **Execution**: Development follows the PR-first workflow defined in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
3. **Review**: Visual verification and walkthroughs at the end of each sprint.
4. **Retrospective**: Continuous process improvement documented in the next sprint planning.
